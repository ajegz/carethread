# Eight-slide presentation

The narrative follows the demonstration: a pending result becomes a clear result in a readback, CareThread makes the disagreement visible, participants clarify responsibility, and acknowledgment preserves the unfinished task.

| Slide | Main point | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CareThread preserves responsibility through a handoff | Large wordmark and concise subtitle on white |
| 2 | A readback can change uncertainty into certainty | Two short, editable quotations with generous space |
| 3 | Open work retains ownership and its source statement | Large, real capture of the redesigned app |
| 4 | Acceptance and clinical completion have separate states | Native comparison or a distinct real accepted-state capture |
| 5 | AssemblyAI speech and Gateway planning feed validated state | Flat three-stage explanation with editable text |
| 6 | Educators and simulation centers are the initial customer hypothesis | Sparse typography with concrete use case and business hypothesis |
| 7 | 80 checks and one synthetic spoken fixture support bounded implementation claims | Large evidence counts with their denominators and limitations |
| 8 | The receiving person can review what remains unfinished | Wordmark, closing sentence and verified project links |

## Poster

1920 × 1080. Minimal CareThread wordmark, “Clinical handoffs with responsibility intact,” a short communication-simulation disclosure, and restrained cobalt. Match the final UI's typography, color and spacing. Keep poster text native and editable in the presentation cover. No Apple logo or integration claim.

## Copy and verification boundaries

- Use 80 software checks, not 80 clinical scenarios.
- Describe one synthetic audio fixture through the real AssemblyAI pipeline, not a human microphone recording.
- Preserve the difference between acknowledgment and reported completion.
- Treat the subscription model and customer demand as hypotheses.
- Use a verified public application URL only after deployment is checked.
- Keep clinical efficacy and browser microphone-device testing explicitly unvalidated.

The final reference may change composition and colors. The narrative and evidence remain fixed unless new verified results arrive.
